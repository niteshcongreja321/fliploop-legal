Know your real profit on every flip.

Fliploop is an iPhone app for resellers. Track your inventory, costs, sales, fees and mileage in one place, and see what you actually made.

  • Inventory Log each item with its category, condition, status, storage location, notes and photos.
  • Costs, sales and fees Record what you paid, what it sold for and where, plus fees and business expenses.
  • Mileage Log your business trips next to your sales and costs.
  • Private by design No ads and no trackers. Your records, photos and receipts are backed up privately to your own account.
